Laundry Presets: Wash + Dry

Accordion PWA • Small GE HE washer • Drain-line suds workaround included • Updated: May 06, 2026

New in this version: Everyday Darks - Moderate Sweat / Daily Wear, plus high-pile polyester blanket routines.
1. Hot Whites - Heavily Used
White socks and frequently used bath towels.
›

Wash

›
CycleWhites / Heavy / Normal
TemperatureHot (use Sanitize only for illness/contamination loads)
SpinExtra-High
DetergentTide Advanced Power Oxi
Amount2 to 2.5 tsp (small HE load)
BleachChlorine bleach: 4 to 5 tsp. Use bleach dispenser. Do not combine with Oxi powder.
Extra RinseYes. Recommended due to bleach odor/residue risk.
Washer Quirk FixRun Drain & Spin after the wash, then run Extra Rinse + High spin.
AvoidLysol sanitizer (not needed with hot wash + bleach). Do not add vinegar.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatHot
Dry LevelFully dry
NotesShake items before drying. Clean lint filter for maximum airflow.

3. Hot Darks - Heavily Soiled
Underwear and sleep T-shirts worn multiple nights (all cotton).
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal / Heavy
TemperatureHot
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Advanced Power Oxi (preferred for body soil/odor)

Dry

4. Everyday Darks - Moderate Sweat / Daily Wear
Black jeans, sweatshirts, ordinary dark T-shirts, and moderately sweaty daily-wear items where cleanliness matters, but preserving dark pigment still matters somewhat.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureCool or Cold
SpinMedium or High; High preferred for jeans and sweatshirts
DetergentTide Free & Gentle preferred overall; Woolite All Darks is also acceptable when soil is lighter and color preservation matters more.
Amount1.5 to 2 tsp
Extra RinseOptional for larger/heavier loads or if you see suds.
AvoidRoutine Lysol sanitizer use; avoid Tide Advanced Power Oxi unless there is unusual odor or staining.
NotesThis is the middle-ground category between Hot Darks - Heavily Soiled and Cold Darks - Nice Clothing.
WhyCleans substantially better than Delicate/Low-Spin dark washing, while preserving dark pigment much better than warm/hot washing.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium-Low
Dry LevelDry, but do not overdry.
RemoveRemove promptly.
NotesDryer heat is one of the biggest causes of fading in dark garments; Medium-Low is the compromise setting.
5. Cold Darks - Nice Clothing
Black tees and pigment-sensitive garments you want to preserve.
›

Wash

›
CycleDelicate / Gentle
TemperatureCold
SpinLow
DetergentWoolite All Darks
Amount2 tsp
PrepTurn items inside out. Do not overload.
AvoidBleach, Oxi powder, and Lysol sanitizer (all increase fading or residue risk).

Dry

›
Dryer HeatLow
Dry LevelDamp-Dry (finish air-drying)
NotesLow heat is the single biggest win for color and fabric longevity.

Wash

›

Dry

›
PillowcasesLow heat; remove slightly damp to reduce creasing; finish air-dry.
SweatersDo not tumble dry. Lay flat to dry.

10. Sanitize Load - Illness / Contamination Event
Use for ordinary washable items when someone is sick, or for fecal contamination/pet accidents/mold exposure textiles.
›

Wash

›
CycleSanitize if available; otherwise Heavy/Normal with Sanitize temperature
TemperatureSanitize
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle or Tide Advanced Power Oxi

Dry LevelFully dry

11. High-Pile Polyester Blankets - Ideal Ongoing Care
Ultra-soft, thick-pile polyester dog blankets where preserving the cloud-like texture matters.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureWarm
SpinHigh
Load Size1 to 2 blankets max; avoid packing the drum.
DetergentTide Free & Gentle
Amount1 to 1.5 tsp max
Extra RinseYes, every time.
Washer Quirk FixRun Drain & Spin before the extra rinse when possible.
Maintenance ResetEvery 3 to 5 washes: warm wash with no detergent + 1 tbsp white vinegar in the fabric softener dispenser, then extra rinse.
AvoidToo much detergent, dryer sheets, frequent sanitize temperature, high-heat drying, bleach, and Oxi unless truly needed for staining.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatLow or Medium-Low
Dry LevelJust dry; do not overdry.
RemoveRemove promptly.
FinishFluff the pile by hand after drying to restore fiber separation.
NotesThe goal is preserving fiber slip; residue and high heat make the pile clump or feel sticky.

Brand-new high-pile polyester blanket straight out of the package; removes factory residue without coating the pile.
›

Wash

›

SpinHigh

Extra RinseYes.

Dry

›

Dry LevelJust dry or slightly early.
FinishFluff by hand thoroughly after drying.
NotesThis first wash helps set the pile up to stay cloud-like long-term.
13. High-Pile Polyester Blankets - Fecal / Vomit Contamination
Use for these specific soft polyester blankets after a dog diarrhea, fecal, or vomit incident.
›

Wash

›
Pre-CleanRemove solids first. Rinse the spot under running warm water and blot; do not grind contamination into the pile.
CycleNormal
TemperatureWarm, not Hot/Sanitize
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle preferred
Amount1.5 tsp
SanitizerYes: Lysol Laundry Sanitizer in the fabric softener compartment, per label; for one blanket, slightly underfilling is reasonable.
Extra RinseRequired.
Washer Quirk FixRun Drain & Spin, then Extra Rinse with High spin.
Use Oxi Only IfVisible staining remains after the first wash; otherwise avoid Tide Oxi/Power Oxi because it raises residue risk.
AvoidSanitize temperature, bleach, dryer sheets, and extra detergent.
If Odor LingersRun a second warm wash with no detergent + 1 tbsp vinegar in the softener compartment, then extra rinse.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatLow or Medium-Low
Dry LevelJust dry.
RemoveRemove promptly.
FinishFluff by hand after drying.
NotesMedium drying is acceptable if needed, but avoid high heat and avoid overdrying.
