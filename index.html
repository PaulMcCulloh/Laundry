Laundry Presets: Wash + Dry

Accordion edition • Small GE HE washer • Drain-line suds workaround included • Updated: February 14, 2026

Tip: This file works offline. Save it to your device and open in Chrome for fastest navigation.

1. Hot Whites - Heavily Used
White socks and frequently used bath towels.
›

Wash

›
CycleWhites / Heavy / Normal
TemperatureHot (use Sanitize only for illness/contamination loads)
SpinExtra-High
DetergentTide Advanced Power Oxi
Amount2 to 2.5 tsp (small HE load)
BleachChlorine bleach: 4 to 5 tsp. Use bleach dispenser. Do not combine with Oxi powder.
Extra RinseYes. Recommended due to bleach odor/residue risk.
Washer Quirk FixRun Drain & Spin after the wash, then run Extra Rinse + High spin.
AvoidLysol sanitizer (not needed with hot wash + bleach). Do not add vinegar.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatHot
Dry LevelFully dry
NotesShake items before drying. Clean lint filter for maximum airflow.
2. Hot Whites - Lightly Soiled / Rare Use
Guest towels, backup linens that are basically clean.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureHot
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle
Amount1.5 to 2 tsp
BleachOptional: 3 to 4 tsp only if whitening is needed.
Extra RinseOnly if bleach odor remains or if skin is sensitive.
AvoidLysol sanitizer; heavy enzyme/oxi detergents are unnecessary for this load.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium-High
Dry LevelDry (avoid Extra Dry)
NotesOverdrying makes towels stiff and shortens pile life.
3. Hot Darks - Heavily Soiled
Underwear and sleep T-shirts worn multiple nights (all cotton).
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal / Heavy
TemperatureHot
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Advanced Power Oxi (preferred for body soil/odor)
Amount1.5 to 2 tsp
Extra RinseOnly if you see suds in the rinse or feel residue.
AvoidBleach; Lysol sanitizer (hot water already covers hygiene).

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium
Dry LevelDry
NotesRemove promptly to reduce wrinkles and elastic stress.
4. Cold Darks - Nice Clothing
Black tees and pigment-sensitive garments you want to preserve.
›

Wash

›
CycleDelicate / Gentle
TemperatureCold
SpinLow
DetergentWoolite All Darks
Amount2 tsp
PrepTurn items inside out. Do not overload.
AvoidBleach, Oxi powder, and Lysol sanitizer (all increase fading or residue risk).

Dry

›
Dryer HeatLow
Dry LevelDamp-Dry (finish air-drying)
NotesLow heat is the single biggest win for color and fabric longevity.
5. Warm / Delicate (Darks or Lights)
800-thread-count cotton pillowcases, delicate cottons, and sweaters.
›

Wash

›
CycleDelicate
TemperatureWarm (or Cold if label requires)
SpinLow
DetergentRegular Woolite (or Tide Free & Gentle for non-wool items if needed)
Amount1.5 to 2 tsp
AvoidBleach, Oxi powder, and Lysol sanitizer.
NotesUse a mesh bag for sweaters if you have one.

Dry

›
PillowcasesLow heat; remove slightly damp to reduce creasing; finish air-dry.
SweatersDo not tumble dry. Lay flat to dry.
6. Warm White - Allergy / Medical Items
Hypoallergenic pillow covers and similar items when you want extra flushing.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureWarm
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle
Amount2 tsp
Extra RinseYes.
Washer Quirk FixRun Drain & Spin before the extra rinse to clear retained suds/soap.
BleachUsually avoid. If needed: 3 to 4 tsp max.
SanitizerUse only if you must wash cold/warm for a hygiene reason and cannot use hot/sanitize.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium-High
Dry LevelFully dry
NotesAvoid dryer sheets (can irritate sensitive skin).
7. Dog Blankets (Polyester) - Allergen Control Mode
After flare-ups, scratching episodes, or suspected allergen exposure.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureHot
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle
Amount2 to 2.5 tsp
AvoidBleach and Lysol sanitizer (not needed here). Avoid dryer sheets.
NotesClean the lint filter before running the washer/dryer cycle set.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium-High (or Hot if needed)
Dry LevelFully dry
NotesFull drying is part of allergen control; shake blankets before loading.
8. Dog Blankets (Polyester) - Normal Heavy Soil
Daily-use blankets that get quite dirty, but no special allergy event.
›

Wash

›
CycleNormal
TemperatureWarm or Hot
SpinHigh
DetergentTide Free & Gentle (default) or Tide Advanced Power Oxi (low dose)
Amount2 tsp (or 1.5 tsp if using Power Oxi)
AvoidBleach; Lysol sanitizer unless illness/contamination.
NotesDo not overload; soil removal needs water exchange in HE machines.

Dry

›
Dryer HeatMedium
Dry LevelDry
NotesMedium heat avoids polyester glazing and preserves softness.

›
